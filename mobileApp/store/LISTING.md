## Full description

TikTak TV is an independent media client built for the services and sources you already use.

Bring your profiles and collections to Android, continue watching across devices, browse enriched movie and series information, connect your own live TV provider and guide, and access your connected YouTube experience from one touch-friendly interface.

Features include:

- Synced profiles, collections, preferences, and playback progress
- Movie and series discovery with rich metadata
- Live TV from your own compatible provider, including program-guide browsing
- YouTube videos, music, subscriptions, playlists, and history when connected
- Fullscreen playback and Picture in Picture
- Phone and tablet layouts with portrait and landscape support
- Playlist, settings, and backup import tools
- Native Android downloads for compatible prepared media
- Privacy controls and service health checks

TikTak TV does not provide channels, subscriptions, add-ons, or media. Connect only services and content you are authorized to use. Availability depends on your providers and configured sources.

TikTak TV is independent and is not affiliated with Google, YouTube, Nuvio, IPTV providers, or streaming services unless expressly stated.
