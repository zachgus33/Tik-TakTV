Delete your TikTak TV account

In the Amazon Fire TV app, sign in and open Settings > Account > Delete account. Confirm the request to delete your Nuvio account and its synchronized account data, including profiles, preferences, collections, and playback progress. Local account data on that device is also cleared.

Request help with deletion

If you cannot access the app, email TikTak TV support from the address associated with your account. Include “TikTak TV account deletion request” in the subject. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deleting data. Do not send your password or provider credentials.

Delete selected data while keeping your account

You can remove an individual profile from the profile management screen and disconnect YouTube from its account settings. To request deletion of other specific TikTak TV account data while keeping your account, email TikTak TV support from your account address and describe the data you want removed.

Third-party services and media providers manage their own accounts and data. Disconnect those services or contact them directly for their deletion options.
