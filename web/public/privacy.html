TikTak TV Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

TikTak TV is a media client. It does not sell personal information or host video content.

Account and service data

When you sign in, TikTak TV uses your Nuvio account to synchronize profiles, preferences, collections, playback progress, and configured add-ons. Provider credentials and configuration may be stored in your browser or encrypted by the TikTak TV companion service so requested features can work.

Google and YouTube data

If you connect YouTube, TikTak TV requests read-only access to your YouTube account. It uses that access to show your subscriptions, recommendations, watch history, playlists, and library. Google refresh tokens are encrypted, associated with your authenticated TikTak TV account, and are not sold or shared for advertising. You can disconnect YouTube from TikTak TV at any time.

Playback and diagnostics

Direct streams travel between your device and the provider. Sources requiring compatibility processing pass through the companion service. Diagnostics omit passwords, provider keys, manifest URLs, and playable stream URLs.

Anonymous usage counts

TikTak TV sends optional privacy-safe usage events containing a random installation identifier and coarse app type so the owner can see aggregate active-device and visit counts. The identifier is hashed by the server. This record does not contain your email, provider configuration, searches, titles, channels, or media URLs. You can disable usage analytics under Settings > Privacy & status.

Signed-in account counts

When optional usage analytics are enabled and you use TikTak TV while signed in, the service records a one-way hash of your Nuvio account ID and browser session, the coarse app type, recent interaction or playback activity, activity dates, session duration, and the account creation time supplied by the authenticated session. It also adds player opens, successful starts, final errors, and elapsed playing-time buckets to global counters grouped by app type and release. These counters do not include titles, channels, URLs, searches, or viewing history and are not shown as a per-account activity log. Only the owner account can view the aggregate dashboard.

Storage and deletion

Clearing browser site data or app storage removes local caches. Disconnecting YouTube deletes its stored account token from TikTak TV. You can also revoke access from your Google Account permissions page. In the Amazon Appstore version, signed-in users can request account deletion from Settings > Account > Delete account. This deletes the Nuvio account and clears local account data. For help with a deletion request, contact the support address listed on TikTak TV's Amazon Appstore page.

Contact

Questions and requests can be submitted through the TikTak TV issue tracker.
