TikTak TV Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

TikTak TV is a media client. It does not sell personal information or host video content.

Account and service data

When you sign in, TikTakTV uses your TikTakTV account to synchronize profiles, preferences, collections, playback progress, watched status, and configured add-ons. Provider credentials and configuration may be stored with the selected profile and cached in your browser so requested features can work across your devices.

Google and YouTube data

If you connect YouTube, TikTak TV requests read-only access to your YouTube account. It uses that access only to provide user-facing features inside TikTak TV, including your channel, subscriptions, playlists, recent account activity, library, and personalized video and music feeds. TikTak TV cannot upload, edit, rate, comment on, or delete content from your YouTube account.

Google user data is not sold, transferred to advertising platforms or data brokers, used for personalized advertising, or read by people except when necessary for security, legal compliance, or support that you explicitly request. TikTak TV's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google data protection and security

TikTak TV protects Google user data with technical and organizational security controls. Google access and refresh tokens are transmitted only over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections between your device, the TikTak TV companion service, and Google's OAuth and YouTube API services. Tokens are handled by the companion service and are not exposed in the TikTak TV user interface.

At rest, Google access and refresh tokens are stored in an authenticated AES-256-GCM encrypted vault. The encryption key is kept separately from the encrypted vault data. Requests for connected YouTube data require a valid TikTak TV account session, and the authenticated account identifier restricts each request to that account's stored credentials. Access is limited to service processes and authorized personnel who need it for security, legal compliance, or support requested by the user.

TikTak TV does not include Google tokens in application diagnostics. If TikTak TV becomes aware of unauthorized access to Google user data, it will investigate and contain the issue, revoke affected credentials when appropriate, and notify affected users or authorities as required by law.

Optional Trakt connection

TikTak TV uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt. Trakt is disconnected by default. If you choose to connect, TikTak TV requests access to your own Trakt account and displays your watchlist. Web Trakt tokens are stored with the selected TikTakTV profile and cached in your browser so the connection follows that profile across devices. Disconnecting removes the profile connection and local token and asks Trakt to revoke the access token.

Playback and diagnostics

Direct streams travel between your device and the provider. Sources requiring compatibility processing pass through the companion service. Diagnostics omit passwords, provider keys, manifest URLs, and playable stream URLs.

Anonymous usage counts

TikTak TV sends optional privacy-safe usage events containing a random installation identifier and coarse app type so the owner can see aggregate active-device and visit counts. The identifier is hashed by the server. This record does not contain your email, provider configuration, searches, titles, channels, or media URLs. You can disable usage analytics under Settings > Privacy & status.

Signed-in account counts

When optional usage analytics are enabled and you use TikTak TV while signed in, the service records a one-way hash of your TikTakTV account ID and browser session, the coarse app type, recent interaction or playback activity, activity dates, session duration, and the account creation time supplied by the authenticated session. It also adds player opens, successful starts, final errors, and elapsed playing-time buckets to global counters grouped by app type and release. These counters do not include titles, channels, URLs, searches, or viewing history and are not shown as a per-account activity log. Only the owner account can view the aggregate dashboard.

Storage and deletion

Clearing browser site data or app storage removes local caches. Disconnecting YouTube deletes TikTak TV's stored Google access and refresh tokens, clears connected-account caches, and prevents further YouTube account access. You can also revoke access from your Google Account permissions page.

To request deletion of any other Google or YouTube data associated with your TikTak TV account, use the contact method below. TikTak TV will delete stored data covered by the request as soon as possible and within seven calendar days. Deleting data from TikTak TV does not delete data held by YouTube.

In the Amazon Appstore version, signed-in users can request account deletion from Settings > Account > Delete account. This deletes the TikTakTV account and clears local account data. For help with a deletion request, contact the support address listed on TikTak TV's Amazon Appstore page. See our account deletion instructions.
